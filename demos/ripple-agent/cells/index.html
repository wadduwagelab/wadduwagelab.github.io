Cell tracking viewer

What did the model label, and what did the tracker learn from it? Claude Opus marked cells in three unlabelled videos and followed them with no human clicks. CoTracker3 was fine-tuned on those tracks and tested on three other videos. This page plays all six videos with the tracks drawn on them. The numbers and the method are on the demo page.

The video frames and reference tracks come from the Cell Tracking Challenge (Ulman et al., Nature Methods 2017; Maška et al., Nature Methods 2023). They are shown here for co-authors to review. Do not copy them or pass them on.
