Cell tracking viewer

What did the model label, and what did the tracker learn from it? Claude Opus marks cells in unlabelled videos and follows them with no human clicks, and CoTracker3 is fine-tuned on its marks. This page plays three development cell videos and the jellyfish video with the model's marks drawn on them, and the three development test videos with each tracker's tracks. The cell marks are those of the current method: the model follows 99 cells, each division is narrowed to one frame, and the tracker trains on the marks alone. That method scores 53.8 on the development test videos; the demo page still describes the earlier version (78 cells, 49.4). A final test on six new datasets is running; its videos are not shown here.

The frames and reference tracks of the cell videos come from the Cell Tracking Challenge (Ulman et al., Nature Methods 2017; Maška et al., Nature Methods 2023). They are shown here for co-authors to review. Do not copy them or pass them on. The jellyfish video is the lab's own.
