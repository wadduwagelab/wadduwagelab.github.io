Research

Designing optics and algorithms together, and making sure the AI we build for science can be trusted.

Differentiable microscopy & optical computing

We treat light propagation as a differentiable program, so microscopes and optical processors can be co-designed with the algorithms that read them out — from quantization-aware optical neurons to volumetric diffractive networks.

Fast imaging through scattering

We combine patterned excitation, multiphoton microscopy and deep learning to image deeper and faster in living tissue, with applications in neuroscience and cell biology.

Trustworthy AI for science

We develop tests that reveal when AI models succeed for the wrong reasons, and verification-first workflows for AI-built scientific software, so biomedical results can be trusted.
